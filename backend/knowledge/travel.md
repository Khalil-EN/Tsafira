# Travel Planning

Travel planning normally involves choosing a destination, determining the dates of travel, planning transportation, selecting accommodation and preparing activities.

# Montreal

Montreal is a major city in Quebec.

Popular areas and attractions include Old Montreal, Mount Royal, the Old Port and downtown Montreal.

Montreal is known for its combination of French and English language communities, restaurants, cultural events and festivals.

# Quebec City

Quebec City is the capital of Quebec.

The historic district of Old Quebec is known for its historic architecture and fortified city walls.

The city is a common destination for visitors interested in history, architecture and culture.

# Travel Preparation

Before travelling, users should check the entry requirements applicable to their destination, verify transportation details and confirm accommodation information.

Travellers should also consider weather conditions, local transportation and their planned activities.

# Travel Itinerary

A simple itinerary can be organized by day.

Each day can contain planned activities, transportation information, meals and free time.

An itinerary should avoid scheduling too many activities in a short period.

# Budget

A travel budget can include transportation, accommodation, food, activities and local transportation.

A useful approach is to estimate each category separately and calculate a total expected cost.

# Safety

Travel advice should be general and should not replace official information from governments, transportation providers or local authorities.

For current travel requirements, users should consult official government and transportation sources.
